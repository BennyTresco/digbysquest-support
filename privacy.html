Privacy policy

Digby's Quest · last updated 2 November 2026

Digby's Quest collects nothing about you and sends nothing anywhere. The game works entirely on your device.

What the game stores

Your progress is saved on your device and stays there:

  • which levels you have won and with how many stars
  • your coins, the power-ups you carry and the looks you have won
  • which chests you have opened and which look you are wearing
  • your music and sound-effect volume settings

That is the whole list. None of it identifies you, and none of it leaves the device.

What the game does not do

  • It does not ask for your name, email or any other detail.
  • It does not use analytics or crash reporting.
  • It does not show advertising or use an advertising identifier.
  • It does not track you across other apps or websites.
  • It does not include any third-party software that collects data.
  • It does not access your contacts, photos, camera, microphone or location.

Children

The game collects no personal information from anyone, of any age.

Purchases

This version of Digby's Quest has no in-app purchases. Coins are earned only by playing.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of the game collects anything, this page will say so before that version is released.
