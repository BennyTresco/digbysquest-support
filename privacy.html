Privacy policy

Digby's Quest · last updated 9 November 2026 · applies from version 1.1

Your progress stays on your device. From version 1.1 the game shows ads from Google, and those ads collect some data. How much is up to you. There is no account and no sign-in, and I never see who you are.

What the game stores on your device

  • which levels you have won and with how many stars
  • your coins, the power-ups you carry and the looks you have won or bought
  • which chests you have opened and which look you are wearing
  • which purchases have been delivered, and whether you have bought Remove Ads
  • your music and sound-effect volume settings

The app keeps a second copy of this on the device so an update can never lose your progress. None of it leaves the device, and I cannot see any of it. Deleting the app deletes it.

Advertising

Digby's Quest is free and is paid for by ads served by Google AdMob: a full-screen ad now and then as you leave a finished level (never during one), and an optional ad you can choose to watch for an extra heart.

To show and measure those ads, Google's software in the app may collect:

  • your device's advertising identifier, only if you allow tracking when iOS asks
  • the type of device, its operating system and language
  • your approximate location, worked out from your internet connection (never GPS)
  • which ads were shown and whether you tapped them, and basic diagnostics such as crashes in the ad software

Google uses this as described in its own policy: how Google uses information from apps that use its services. I receive only totals (how many ads were shown and what they earned), never anything about you personally.

Your choices

  • Tracking. iOS asks whether Digby's Quest may track you. If you say no, ads are still shown but are not based on your activity in other apps. You can change it any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • Europe, the UK and Switzerland. You are asked for consent before any personal data is used for ads. You can change your answer any time with Privacy Choices in the game's settings (the cog).
  • Some US states. You can opt out of the sale or sharing of your data with the same Privacy Choices button.
  • Remove Ads. Buying it turns off the ads between levels for good. The extra-heart ad stays available, but only ever plays if you choose to watch it.

Purchases

Coins, Remove Ads and the Royal Treasurer are bought through Apple. Apple handles the payment entirely; I never see your name, card or Apple ID, only that a purchase was made.

Update check

When the game starts it reads one small file from this website to find out whether a newer version is available. No information about you or your progress is sent. Like any website, the host (GitHub) may briefly log the request.

What the game does not do

  • It does not ask for your name, email or any other detail.
  • It does not use analytics of its own.
  • It does not access your contacts, photos, camera, microphone or precise location.
  • It does not sell anything about you. The data above is collected by Google for advertising, under the choices described above.

Children

Digby's Quest is rated 9+ but is not made for children, and ads in it are limited to ones suitable for a general audience. I do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to this policy

If what the game collects changes, this page will say so plainly, and the date at the top will change.
